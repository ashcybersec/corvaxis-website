Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

Corvaxis Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales. Our website address is https://corvaxis.co.uk.

What data we collect

We only collect data that you voluntarily provide to us through our contact form:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name (optional)
  • The content of your message

How we use your data

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry. We will not use your data for marketing purposes unless you explicitly opt in.

Data storage and third parties

Contact form submissions are processed and stored via Web3Forms, a third-party service. We do not store your data on our own servers. Web3Forms retains submission data in accordance with their own privacy policy. We recommend you review their policy for full details.

We do not share, sell, or trade your personal data with any other third parties.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for a maximum of 12 months. After this period, all personal data is permanently deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Request data portability

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@corvaxis.co.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any tracking technologies. We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party trackers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please email hello@corvaxis.co.uk.
